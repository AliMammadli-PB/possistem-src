# Surface: QR order menu (`/qr/<menu>/<table>/`)

Mode: Operate. Guest at a restaurant table, phone in one hand, orders from the till's live menu.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The guest's table is the page. The menu starts in the first screen and every chosen dish lands on "Masa 7 süfrəsi", the table's own spread at the foot of the screen. Refuses the category default: a hero banner over a grid of equal photo cards with letter placeholders.

OWN-WORLD: A laid table. Linen ground (warm off-white, tinted from the restaurant's accent), deep ink type, the restaurant's accent as the cloth's band (header strip, active category, plates on the spread, primary button) and nowhere else. Plates are round: dish photos sit in circles, and chosen dishes appear as overlapping round plates on the spread bar. Prices in one tabular right-aligned column. Hairline rules between dishes, not boxed cards.

STORY: The guest sees the restaurant's name, logo and their table, scans categories, adds dishes (with options when needed), watches them land on the table, opens the spread, checks the total and sends it to the till; then follows its status.

FIRST VIEWPORT (390 px): sticky header (logo, name, language) with the accent band carrying "Masa 7 · Terras"; sticky category tabs with search; first category heading and its first four dishes as rows (round photo or none, name, one-line description, price, add button). The spread bar sits at the bottom once something is chosen. Cover photo, when the owner set one, is a short strip under the header, never a slider.

FORM: Süfrə (laid table), position 7 of the grounded list; seed: 9f7c37a8c7f57193. Raises: from the split-flap board, an unavailable dish restyles in place without breaking the column; from the cassette j-card, prices right-aligned in tabular figures and the total always visible; from the character catalog, every dish keeps the same fixed slots with or without a photo; from the orienteering map, the accent is reserved for what is active or chosen.

# Restaurant POS 1.10.20

Windows installer: `https://possistem.az/pos-updates/Possistem-Setup-1.10.20.exe`.
Download page: `https://possistem.az/download/pos/`.
Version is consistent in package/lock, CMake and Electron builder configuration.

The assistant now reads current warehouse quantities, tables/orders, sales and cash accounts, and uses the account-bound billing status/handoff. Word-order variants and low-stock questions are supported. Relative stock increases/decreases are distinct from setting a final quantity; exact dish names are preferred over weaker variant matches. A correction waiting for a reason survives an inventory overview. Raw material and menu creation, and new recipes with real ingredients/per-portion quantities, use a guided draft and explicit confirmation. No recipe ingredients or selling prices are guessed. Copy-message/copy-conversation controls and an accessible visible thinking indicator are included.

The new native core advertises protected assistant tools version 1. Card patches are narrow, permission-checked, versioned and audited. Quantity adjustments compare the expected warehouse balance and retain idempotency. Thus the old 1.10.19 preview's chat-write restriction is removed in this build. The tested native scenario creates raw material, a priced menu item and a 250 g recipe; then verifies confirmed stock/price changes, replay, stale-stock rejection and preservation of unrelated fields.

Financial reconciliation fixes retain original settled tender rows after refunds and split mixed cash/card tender totals correctly in X/Z, period and shift reports. Cash tips/cancelled expenses are accounted for once. Business-day close/Z retries are idempotent across restart and do not close a newer day. A mixed-tender refund is explicitly blocked until safe allocation is implemented. Payment loading failures have retry UI; users without payment.take do not see the payment action. Existing user-requested navigation, compact sale grid, floor/delivery layout and transparent PNG changes are included.

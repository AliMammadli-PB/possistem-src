# Restaurant POS 1.9.21 — immediate trial access

A password-authenticated POS session previously left on the payment screen can now resume after an administrator grants access. The new `/tenant/resume` endpoint reuses normal customer, product and hardware checks, accepts only an unexpired billing session, preserves its expiry, and audits the transition. Customer deactivation, password epochs, logout revocations, license revocation and seat limits remain enforced. Hardware allocation is serialized so two computers cannot claim the same final seat.

The Restaurant login transport now forwards its product to the API. Previously the wrapper accepted a product argument but omitted it from the request. Periodic and manual access checks import the new signed license, update the protected local session, and reload once after resumption. An account change while a check is in flight cannot open the former account. Access cache lifetime is bounded by trial and signed-license expiry.

The PIN screen and application show a compact free-trial notice with remaining days/hours and the expiry date. The payment screen identifies the account and shows device/seat errors from resumption. A grant remains scoped to the customer and product chosen by the administrator; this release does not grant another customer access or fabricate payment records.
